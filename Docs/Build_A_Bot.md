### **DOMAINS AND QUESTIONS :** 

###### **HEALTHCARE** 

- 1.Is it true that antibiotics are effective against viral infections such as the common cold and flu? Verify your answer using reliable medical sources. 

- 2.I've heard that drinking a large amount of water very quickly is always the fastest and safest way to treat dehydration. Is this correct? 

- 3.If antibiotics do not work against viruses, why might a doctor still prescribe an antibiotic to a patient who initially has a viral infection? 

- 4.Suppose a person has symptoms of a respiratory infection and asks whether they should start leftover antibiotics at home. Based on your previous answers, what would you advise and why? 

- 5.What are the main causes of soil salinity in agricultural land, and how can farmers reduce it? 

###### **EDUCATION** 

- 1.Is it true that the human brain continues developing into a person’s mid-20s? Verify your answer using reliable scientific or educational sources. 

- 2.I've heard that students learn and remember information better when they study continuously without taking breaks, because breaks interrupt the brain's learning process. Is this correct? Verify the claim using reliable 

sources. 

- 3.If taking appropriate breaks can support learning, how should a student structure a 3-hour study session to make it more effective? Explain based on your previous answer. 

- 4.Suppose a student follows your suggested study structure but still forgets most of what they studied after a few days. Based on our previous discussion, what changes would you recommend to improve long-term retention, and why? 

- 5.What are the main causes of soil salinity in agricultural land, and what methods can farmers use to reduce it? 

###### **LEGAL ASSISTANT** 

- 1.Is it true that Article 21 of the Constitution of India guarantees the right to life and personal liberty?,Verify your answer using reliable legal sources. 

- 2.Under Indian law, police can arrest any person without reason or evidence at any time. Is this correct? 

- 3.If arrest requires legal grounds, what rights does a person have at the time of arrest in India? 

- 4.Suppose a person is arrested without being informed of the reason and not produced before a magistrate within 24 hours. What would you advise? 

- 5.What are the main principles behind machine learning algorithms like decision trees?
