PROJECT REGISTRATION & COMPLIANCE:
1. Mandatory Registration: Mandatory registration with the State RERA authority for all commercial and residential real estate projects where land area exceeds 500 square meters or number of apartments exceeds 8.
2. Escrow Account Rule: Promoters must deposit 70% of funds realized from allottees into a dedicated bank account to cover construction and land costs.
3. Information Disclosure: Promoters must publicly declare sanctioned plans, layout plans, implementation schedules, and legal title status on the RERA portal.

BUILDER DELAY COMPENSATION & STRUCTURAL DEFECT LIABILITY:
1. Delay Interest & Refund: In case of delay in handing over possession, allottees have the right to claim a full refund with interest or receive monthly interest payments for every month of delay at statutory rates.
2. Structural Defect Liability (Section 14(3)): Builder is liable to rectify any structural defect or defect in workmanship brought to notice within 5 years from possession date, without extra charge within 30 days.
3. Adherence to Sanctioned Plans: Promoters cannot make additions or alterations to sanctioned plans without the prior written consent of two-thirds of the allottees.
