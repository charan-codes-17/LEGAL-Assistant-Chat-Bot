[TITLE: Model Tenancy Act, 2021 — Eviction Grounds, Security Deposit Caps & Maintenance Protocols]
[AUTHORITY: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India]
[URL: https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-model-tenancy-act-2021]

SECURITY DEPOSIT CAPS & REFUND TIMELINES:
1. Residential Deposit Cap: Maximum security deposit for residential premises is capped at 2 months' rent.
2. Commercial Deposit Cap: Maximum security deposit for non-residential/commercial premises is capped at 6 months' rent.
3. Deposit Refund Window: Landlord must refund the security deposit (minus valid itemized deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear) within 30 days of the tenant vacating possession.

EVICTION NORMS & MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. Grounds for Eviction: Eviction can only be ordered by the Rent Authority on valid grounds, such as failure to pay agreed rent for 2 consecutive months, unauthorized sub-letting, or misuse of premises after written notice.
2. Maintenance Allocation: Structural repairs remain the duty of the landlord, while routine day-to-day maintenance is the responsibility of the tenant, as specified in the mandatory written agreement.
3. Mandatory Notice for Entry: Landlords must provide at least 24 hours' prior written notice before entering the rented premises for inspection or repairs.
