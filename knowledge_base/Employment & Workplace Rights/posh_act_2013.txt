[TITLE: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013]
[AUTHORITY: Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India]
[URL: https://wcd.gov.in/documents/uploaded/1710219823.pdf]

STATUTORY MANDATES & ICC SETUP:
1. Internal Complaints Committee (ICC): Mandatory constitution of an ICC at every workplace/branch employing 10 or more workers, headed by a senior woman employee.
2. Employer Obligations: Duty to display penal consequences, conduct awareness workshops, provide safe environment, and submit annual compliance reports to local authorities.
3. Inquiry & Interim Relief: ICC must complete inquiries within 90 days; employers must provide interim relief (e.g., leave up to 3 months, transfer of aggrieved party or respondent) during inquiry upon ICC recommendation.

COMPLAINT FILING WINDOWS & PROCEDURE:
1. Filing Window: Aggrieved woman must file a written complaint within 3 months from the date of the incident (extendable by ICC up to another 3 months for recorded reasons).
2. Conciliation Option: ICC may initiate conciliation upon request of the aggrieved woman before starting formal inquiry, provided no monetary settlement is proposed.
