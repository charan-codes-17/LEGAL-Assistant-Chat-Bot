NOTICE PERIOD & SEVERANCE PAY CALCULATION:
1. Statutory Notice Period: Mandates 30 to 90 days written notice (or pay in lieu of notice) for retrenchment of workmen in continuous service.
2. Severance Pay (Retrenchment Compensation): Statutory entitlement to 15 days' average pay for every completed year of continuous service or part thereof in excess of 6 months.
3. Protection Against Wrongful Termination: Prohibits unfair labor practices; termination without statutory notice, proper inquiry, or valid operational justification is illegal and subject to reinstatement with back wages.

MATERNITY BENEFIT SAFEGUARDS:
1. Paid Leave Entitlement: Up to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for female employees (with up to 8 weeks before expected delivery date) for the first two children.
2. Retaliation Protection: Strict statutory bar prohibiting dismissal, discharge, or alteration of service conditions of a woman worker during her maternity absence.
3. Workplace Creche Facility: Mandatory provision of creche facilities for establishments employing 50 or more employees.
