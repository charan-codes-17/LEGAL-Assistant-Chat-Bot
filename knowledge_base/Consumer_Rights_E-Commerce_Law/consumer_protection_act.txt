STATUTORY CONSUMER RIGHTS:
1. Right to Protection: Safety against goods, products, or services that are hazardous to life and property.
2. Right to Information: Complete details regarding quality, quantity, potency, purity, standard, and price of goods or services.
3. Right to Access: Assured access to a variety of goods, products, or services at competitive prices.
4. Right to Redressal: Seeking remedies against unfair trade practices, restrictive trade practices, or exploitation.
5. Right to Consumer Awareness: Education regarding consumer rights and statutory protections.

DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS, REFUNDS & MISLEADING ADS:
1. Defect & Deficiency: Right to statutory return, replacement, or full refund for defective goods or deficient services.
2. Unfair Trade Practices (UTP): Strict prohibitions against false representations, fraudulent claims, and refusal to issue cash memos/invoices.
3. Misleading Advertisements: Strict liability for endorsers, manufacturers, and publishers false-claiming product standards or safety metrics.
