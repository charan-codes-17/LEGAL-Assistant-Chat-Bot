MARKETPLACE TRANSPARENCY & SELLER LIABILITY:
1. Mandatory Seller Details: Display of legal entity name, principal geographic address, customer care details, and seller ratings.
2. Non-Discrimination: Prohibition against manipulating search indices or favoring specific sellers to disadvantage consumers.
3. Price & Refund Transparency: Mandatory disclosure of total price broken down alongside break-ups, delivery fees, return policies, and statutory return windows.

GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL TIMELINES:
1. Nodal Officer Appointment: Mandatory designation of a Grievance Officer for handling consumer issues.
2. Acknowledgment Window: Mandatory acknowledgment of any consumer complaint within 48 hours of receipt.
3. Redressal Resolution Timeline: Complete resolution of consumer complaints within 1 month from the date of receipt.
