DATA PRINCIPAL RIGHTS & CONSENT REQUIREMENTS:
1. Free & Informed Consent: Data processing requires specific, unambiguous, and revocable consent from the Data Principal for explicitly defined lawful purposes.
2. Right to Data Erasure & Correction: Entitles users to request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the original processing purpose, along with correction of incomplete or outdated data.
3. Grievance Redressal: Right of individuals to access details of personal data being processed and seek effective grievance resolution mechanisms from entities.

DATA FIDUCIARY OBLIGATIONS & BREACH NOTIFICATION:
1. Personal Data Protection Safeguards: Mandatory implementation of reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches during processing.
2. Breach Notification Mandate: Obligation of Data Fiduciaries to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals immediately in the event of any personal data breach.
3. Data Minimization & Storage Limitation: Mandates deleting personal data as soon as the specified processing purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn.
