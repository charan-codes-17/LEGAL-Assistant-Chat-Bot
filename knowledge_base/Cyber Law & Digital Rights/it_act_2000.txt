CYBER OFFENCES & PENAL PROVISIONS:
1. Unauthorized Access & Hacking (Section 43 & Section 66): Penalizes damaging, downloading, extracting, or gaining unauthorized access to computer systems, data, or networks without permission.
2. Identity Theft (Section 66C): Imposes strict criminal liability for fraudulently or dishonestly using the electronic signature, password, or unique identification feature of any other person.
3. Online Financial Fraud & Cheating (Section 66D): Penalizes cheating by personation by using computer resources or communication devices for fraudulent monetary gain.
4. Privacy Violation & Cyberstalking (Section 66E & Section 67): Punishes intentional violation of personal privacy, transmitting obscene material, or tracking and harassing individuals online without consent.
