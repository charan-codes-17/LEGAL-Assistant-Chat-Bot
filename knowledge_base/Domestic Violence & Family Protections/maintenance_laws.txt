MAINTENANCE STATUTORY PROVISIONS & SCOPE:
1. Statutory Right to Maintenance (CrPC Sec 125 / BNSS Sec 144): Creates a summary statutory remedy compelling individuals with sufficient means to maintain dependents unable to maintain themselves.
2. Protected Dependents: Extends enforceable maintenance rights to:
   - Legally wedded wives (who have not remarried and have not deserted without sufficient cause).
   - Minor legitimate or illegitimate children (whether married or unmarried).
   - Major children with physical/mental abnormality or injury preventing self-support.
   - Aged or infirm fathers and mothers.

INTERIM MAINTENANCE & ENFORCEMENT:
1. Interim Relief Timeline: Applications for interim monthly allowance and proceeding expenses must be disposed of within 60 days from the date of notice service to the respondent.
2. Enforcement & Non-Compliance: Authorizes the court to issue distress warrants for levying fines or order imprisonment up to 1 month for every breach of maintenance orders.
