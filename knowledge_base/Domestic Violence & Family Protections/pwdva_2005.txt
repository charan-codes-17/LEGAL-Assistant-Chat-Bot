[TITLE: Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 — Emergency Orders & Residence Rights]
[AUTHORITY: Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India]

EMERGENCY PROTECTION & RESIDENCE ORDERS:
1. Protection Orders (Section 18): Enables the Magistrate to pass ex-parte emergency orders restraining the respondent from committing, aiding, or abetting acts of domestic violence.
2. Right to Shared Household (Section 17): Grants every woman in a domestic relationship the statutory right to reside in the shared household, regardless of whether she has any legal right, title, or equity in the property.
3. Residence Orders (Section 19): Restrains respondents from dispossessing or disturbing the victim's peaceful possession of the shared household or directs the respondent to secure alternate accommodation.

MONETARY RELIEF & COMPENSATION:
1. Monetary Relief (Section 20): Directs payment to meet medical expenses, loss of earnings, property damage, and daily maintenance needs of the aggrieved person and her children.
2. Custody & Compensation Orders (Section 21 & Section 22): Provides temporary custody of children to the aggrieved person and mandates compensation for physical injuries and emotional distress caused by domestic abuse.[SOURCE_ID: PWDVA-ACT-2005]
