[TITLE: Constitution of India — Article 19: Protection of Certain Rights regarding Freedom of Speech, etc.]
[AUTHORITY: Government of India / Legislative Department]
[URL: https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/c9fe9c9b6840524844316f74bb1c556c.pdf]

TEXT OF PROVISION:
Article 19(1): All citizens shall have the right—
(a) to freedom of speech and expression;
(b) to assemble peaceably and without arms;
(c) to form associations or unions or co-operative societies;
(d) to move freely throughout the territory of India;
(e) to reside and settle in any part of the territory of India; and
(g) to practise any profession, or to carry on any occupation, trade or business.

LEGAL PRINCIPLES AND RESTRICTIONS:
1. Citizen Exclusivity: The rights guaranteed under Article 19 are available only to citizens of India, unlike Articles 14, 20, 21, and 22 which are available to all persons.
2. Reasonable Restrictions: The freedoms under Article 19 are not absolute. The State can impose reasonable restrictions under clauses (2) through (6) on grounds such as:
   - Sovereignty and integrity of India
   - Security of the State
   - Friendly relations with foreign States
   - Public order, decency, or morality
   - Contempt of court, defamation, or incitement to an offence.
3. Procedural Interconnection: Restrictions imposed on individual freedoms must satisfy the test of reasonableness under Article 14 and substantive fairness under Article 21.
